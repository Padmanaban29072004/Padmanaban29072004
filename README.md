## The Story So Far

I didn't get into AI because it was trending. I got into it because I wanted to build things that felt impossibly ambitious — and AI was the tool that kept saying *"yes, you can."*

I'm a final-year CS student in Chennai, and somewhere between reverse-engineering networks, teaching a model to read the Moon's surface, and building a system that detects cyber threats in under 3 milliseconds — I found my groove.

Three projects define me right now:

**🛡️ PHANTOM-Flow** — A multi-agent cybersecurity system that orchestrates AI agents across a live network, flagging anomalies before humans even see the logs. Built with LangChain, LangGraph, and FastAPI. Sub-3ms detection. Real SOC-grade stuff.

**🌕 LunaLens** — I trained YOLOv8 and Vision Transformers on ISRO's Chandrayaan-2 imagery to detect craters and boulders on the lunar surface. This one got me to the finals of **Bharatiya Antariksh 2025** and is headed to ISSF 2026.

**🏙️ UrbanFlow** — Geospatial multi-agent platform for smart city traffic optimization. Because cities shouldn't need humans to untangle rush hour.

I also won **Smart India Hackathon 2025**, which taught me that good ideas under pressure become great systems.
